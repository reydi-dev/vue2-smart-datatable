# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Project participation must remain respectful, constructive, and inclusive. Examples of positive behavior include:

- giving actionable technical feedback;
- respecting different experience levels and viewpoints;
- focusing criticism on code and ideas rather than people;
- accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, and disruptive or abusive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other material that violates these standards. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

To report a serious incident privately, use the repository's private reporting channel under the **Security** tab. Include relevant links and context, but do not disclose another person's sensitive information unnecessarily.
